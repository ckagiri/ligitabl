Why LigiPredictor Exists

"Ligi" means league in Swahili. This is a table-prediction game to see if you really know the Premier League—and prove you're better than the rest.

Most prediction games ask you to pick match winners, one game at a time. That's fine, but it doesn't test what football fans actually argue about — where a team belongs, over a whole season.

LigiPredictor asks a bigger question: can you call the entire table, top to bottom, before that gameweek's matches kick off?

We built the game we wanted to play — one that respects your time as much as your opinions.

Just a table, a countdown, and the chance to prove you called it before anyone else did.
