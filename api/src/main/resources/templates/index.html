How It Works

No daily grind required — every gameweek gives you a fresh window to adjust, no matter how many times you check in a week.

1

🏗️ Build Your Table

Start from the previous gameweek's finish and use up to 5 swaps to reorder it your way — or leave it as-is.

2

🎯 Enter the Competition

Submit your table — even with zero changes — and you're officially in for the season.

ℹ️ Hit Submit — that's the step that actually enters You.
3

🔄 Adjust As You Go

Get 1 swap every 24h, right up until that round's fixtures kick off — then earn a score and chase trophies all season.

The leaderboard ranks by Total Points → Total Perfect Positions → Fewest Swaps Used. Using many swaps helps you climb — but it might cost you a tie-break later.

Leaderboard Phases

Three ways to compete. Something to chase every gameweek.

🥇

Full Season

GW 1 – 38

The prestige trophy. Cumulative across all 38 gameweeks. The earlier you start, the harder it is for others to catch you.

🗓️

Quarters

Q1 · Q2 · Q3 · Q4 — ~9 GWs each

Four distinct competitions across the season. Sustained good performance is what separates the contenders from the rest.

⚡

Sprints

S1 – S8 — ~5 GWs each

Eight short, sharp bursts throughout the season. A single great gameweek can swing it — the most winnable trophy for anyone on a hot streak.

🚀

Join Anytime. Always Something to Chase.

Joined mid-season? Mid-quarter? Even mid-sprint? No problem. Your scores count from day one, and sprint-resets every ~5 gameweeks mean late joiners are always fully competitive in the next one.
