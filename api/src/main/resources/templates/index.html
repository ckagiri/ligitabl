Scoring System

How Points Are Calculated

You start with 200 points each gameweek. For each team, you lose points equal to the position difference.

Perfect prediction (0 positions off) No points lost! 🎯
Close call (1 position off) -1 point
Near miss (3 positions off) -3 points
Big miss (8 positions off) -8 points

Formula: Your Score = Max Points - Total Position Errors

Leaderboard Tiebreakers

When scores are level, here's how we separate you.

1

Total Points

Your cumulative score for the competition period — whether that's a sprint, quarter, or the full season. The primary ranking criterion — highest points wins.

2

Zeroes — worth their weight in gold

The count of perfect predictions — where your predicted position matched the actual position exactly (0 positions off, no points lost). The more of these you accumulate, the better.

3

Best Single Gameweek Score

Your highest scoring gameweek within the phase. Peak performance counts.

4

Fewest Swaps Used

Fewer swaps used to achieve the same score signals a stronger original prediction. Swaps are still encouraged — they're your tool to stay competitive — but efficiency is rewarded at the margin.

5

Internal Tiebreak

In the extremely rare case of a perfect tie across all criteria, an internal identifier is used. This is essentially a coin flip — focus on points and zeroes long before worrying about this.

The goal is simple: more points and more zeroes. Everything else is a footnote.
