How It Works

1

Create your account and rank the teams. Everyone starts from last season's final standings — make 1 to 3 swaps to set your opening table, then submit to join.

2

Adjust your table strategically — swap two teams' positions once every 24 hours while rounds are open. Every swap is a chance to gain an edge.

Scoring & Rankings

How points work, what they mean, and how ties are broken.

How Points Are Calculated

You start with 200 points each gameweek. For each team, you lose points equal to the position difference.

Perfect prediction (0 positions off) No points lost! 🎯
Close call (1 position off) -1 point
Near miss (3 positions off) -3 points
Big miss (8 positions off) -8 points

Formula: Your Score = 200 - Total Position Errors

Improve Inward

Your best gameweek score is your personal record. Every round is a chance to break it — regardless of where you rank. The leaderboard tells you where you stand against others; Your best tells you where you stand against yourself.

🏆

Compete Outward

Climb the leaderboard against everyone else. Sprints, quarters, full season — always something to chase, always someone to beat.

1

Total Points

Your cumulative score for the competition period — sprint, quarter, or full season. Highest wins.

2

Zeroes — worth their weight in gold

Count of perfect predictions — position matched exactly (0 positions off). The more the better.

3

Best Single Gameweek Score

Your highest scoring gameweek within the phase. Peak performance counts.

4

Fewest Swaps Used

Fewer swaps for the same score signals a stronger original prediction. Swaps are still encouraged — efficiency is rewarded at the margin.

5

Internal Tiebreak

In the extremely rare case of a perfect tie across all criteria, an internal identifier is used. Focus on points and zeroes long before worrying about this.

The goal is simple: more points, more zeroes. Everything else is a footnote.
