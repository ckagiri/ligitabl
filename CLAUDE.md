## Remembering things

When asked to remember something, write it to one place and say which:

- **`.agents/MEMORY.md`** (committed): a rule for working in this repo that holds for
  anyone working on it.
- **Your own Claude memory** (outside the repo): everything else. That covers personal
  preferences, work in progress and paths on your machine.

If unsure, use your own memory. A note is easy to move into the repo later and hard
to take out of git history.
